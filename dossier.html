⚡ Important Q&A
If I transfer colleges, does my loan get cancelled?
No! You submit the new college's admission letter & fee structure to your SBI branch. The bank updates the college account details in their system — loan continues smoothly.
Will the bank take my internship income?
No. 100% of your internship stipends and freelance income belong to you during college. Repayment only starts 1 year after you graduate.
Does this loan help my credit score later?
Yes! Paying small on-time EMIs after graduation builds you a 780+ CIBIL score by age 22. This unlocks the lowest interest rates on future home loans, business loans, and credit cards.
What if my course takes extra time (ATKT year)?
Bank extends your moratorium period by 1–2 years automatically. You just submit a 1-page letter from your college stating the revised completion date.
