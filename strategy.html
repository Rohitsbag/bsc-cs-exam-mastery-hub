1. Academic & University Inquiries (Rohit's Doubts)
If I don't write Sem 2 in October, does Mumbai University fail or penalize me?
No. In Mumbai University, when an applied candidate skips an examination paper, the university marksheet simply records `AB` (Absent). It is not an unfair means violation, disciplinary penalty, or permanent demerit. Under the university's carry-forward ordinance, your passed internal marks (CIA) remain completely safe and are carried forward to the March 2027 attempt automatically.
Will my final degree certificate state "Passed in ATKT" or have a red mark?
Never. Mumbai University degree certificates (awarded upon graduation) state: *"Bachelor of Science (Computer Science) - Awarded with Grade [O/A+] / First Class with Distinction"*. Degree certificates never state how many attempts you took or print the word "ATKT". Only the internal semester grade-card shows the month/year of passing that specific unit.
What if I change my mind in October and want to write a Sem 2 paper?
Because you already paid on CIMS (Receipt #86) and submitted your Samarth form, you will have the official hall ticket generated for Sem 2. If you feel like sitting for an easy paper (like Web Technologies or Hindi) without stress, you can literally walk into the hall and write it! If you don't go, it's just marked AB. You hold 100% optionality.
