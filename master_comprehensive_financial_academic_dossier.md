# 2. NEP 2020: 3-YEAR VS. 4-YEAR B.SC. COMPUTER SCIENCE STRUCTURE

Under Mumbai University’s NEP 2020 credit framework, the undergraduate degree features **Multiple Entry & Exit Options**:

```
B.Sc. Computer Science Progression (NEP 2020)
├── Year 1 (Sem 1 & 2): 40 Credits  ──> Exit: Undergraduate Certificate in CS
├── Year 2 (Sem 3 & 4): 80 Credits  ──> Exit: Undergraduate Diploma in CS
├── Year 3 (Sem 5 & 6): 120 Credits ──> Exit: Bachelor of Science (B.Sc. CS Degree)
└── Year 4 (Sem 7 & 8): 160 Credits ──> Exit: B.Sc. (Honours / Honours with Research)
```

### Key Differences:
1. **3-Year Track (120 Credits):** Standard Bachelor's Degree. 100% eligible for software industry jobs, tech placements, MCA, MBA, and government exams.
2. **4-Year Track (160+ Credits):** Honors with Research / Internship. Allows direct admission into a **1-Year Master's Degree (M.Sc. / MS)** or Ph.D. without a 2-year Master's.
3. **Loan Applicability:** Education loans can be taken for either 3 years or 4 years, and can be extended if you choose the 4th-year research track.
